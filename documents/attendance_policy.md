# Attendance Policy

## Check-in

Participants check in at the venue using their registration ID. Check-in opens 30
minutes before the scheduled start time and closes 30 minutes after it.

## Late arrival

A participant arriving after check-in closes may be admitted at the organiser's
discretion, but will be recorded as a late arrival rather than as attended. For
events that issue certificates, late arrival may affect eligibility.

## Attendance record

Attendance is recorded against the registration. Possible outcomes are:

- **attended** — checked in within the check-in window
- **late** — admitted after check-in closed
- **no-show** — registered but did not attend and did not cancel

## Certificates

Where an event offers a certificate of participation, it is issued only to
participants marked as attended. Participants marked late or no-show are not
eligible. Certificates are issued within 14 days of the event.

## Minimum attendance

Events that run across multiple sessions require attendance at a minimum of 75% of
sessions for the event to count as attended.

## Consequences of repeated no-shows

Three no-shows within a calendar year may result in registration privileges being
suspended for 90 days. Cancelling in advance, at any time before the event starts,
never counts as a no-show.
