# Cancellation Policy

## Cancelling your registration

A participant may cancel their own registration at any time before the event
starts. Cancellation releases the seat immediately and makes it available to
other users.

## Refund schedule

Refunds apply only to paid events. Free events have no refund component.

| Cancellation time | Refund |
|---|---|
| More than 7 days before the event | 100% of the fee |
| Between 48 hours and 7 days before | 50% of the fee |
| Less than 48 hours before | No refund |
| After the event has started | No refund |

Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7 to 10 working days.

## No-shows

A participant who neither attends nor cancels is recorded as a no-show. No-shows
receive no refund. Three no-shows within a calendar year may result in registration
privileges being suspended for 90 days.

## Cancellation by the organiser

If an event is cancelled by the organiser, all registered participants receive a
full refund regardless of when the cancellation occurs, and are notified using the
contact details on their account. Organiser cancellation does not count as a
no-show against the participant.

## Rescheduled events

If an event is moved to a new date, existing registrations carry over
automatically. A participant who cannot attend the new date may cancel for a full
refund within 7 days of being notified, regardless of the standard refund schedule.
