# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I find events?

Search by keyword, category or city from the events page, or ask the AI assistant
in plain language, for example "find AI workshops in Hyderabad this month".

## How do I register for an event?

Open the event and select register, or ask the AI assistant to register you. You
must be logged in. Registration is confirmed immediately if seats are available.

## Can I register someone else?

No. Registrations are personal and are always made for the logged-in user. Each
participant must register from their own account.

## What happens if an event is full?

Registration closes when capacity is reached. There is no waitlist at present. If
another participant cancels, the seat is released and can be claimed by anyone.

## How do I cancel?

Cancel from your registrations page, or ask the AI assistant. Cancellation is
immediate and the seat is released straight away. Refunds follow the cancellation
policy schedule.

## Will I get a refund if I cancel?

Only paid events have refunds. The amount depends on how far in advance you
cancel — see the cancellation policy. Free events have nothing to refund.

## What if the organiser cancels the event?

You receive a full refund regardless of timing, and you are notified by email. It
does not count as a no-show against you.

## Can I change which event I am registered for?

Not directly. Cancel the first registration, then register for the other event.
Standard refund rules apply to the cancellation.

## Why can I not create an event?

Event creation is restricted to administrators. If you need an event set up,
contact an event administrator.

## What can the AI assistant do?

It can search events, show event details, check venue availability, register you,
cancel your registration, list what you are signed up for, and answer questions
about these policies. It cannot act on behalf of another user, and administrators
have additional event-management abilities that regular users do not.

## Who do I contact for help?

Use the contact page, or ask an event administrator directly.
