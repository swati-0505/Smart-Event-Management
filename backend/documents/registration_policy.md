# Registration Policy

## Who may register

Any user with a verified account may register for events with `published` status.
Draft and cancelled events are not open for registration.

## Registration limits

A user may hold one active registration per event. Duplicate registration attempts
are rejected by the system. There is no limit on the total number of different
events a user may register for.

## Capacity

Registration closes automatically when an event reaches capacity. Seats are
allocated first come, first served. There is no waitlist in the current release;
if a seat is released by a cancellation it becomes available to whoever claims it
first.

## Registration deadline

Where an event specifies a registration deadline, registration closes at that time
even if seats remain. Where no deadline is set, registration remains open until the
event starts or capacity is reached.

## Confirmation

On successful registration the system issues a registration ID. Participants should
keep this ID; it is required for check-in and for any support query about the booking.

## Transfers

Registrations are personal and may not be transferred to another person. A
participant who cannot attend should cancel so that the seat is released.
